Ghidorah: The Three-Headed Sentinel
"Omniscience without the overhead."

What is Ghidorah?
Ghidorah is a purpose-built, highly opinionated telemetry engine for Kubernetes. While platforms like Grafana give you a blank canvas to build anything, Ghidorah gives you the exact instrument you need to monitor one thing perfectly: your cluster. It is designed to work completely out of the box, offering a high-density, tactical visualization of your infrastructure with absolute zero configuration.

Our Philosophy

Opinionated by Design: We reject the blank slate. You shouldn't have to write PromQL queries, import community dashboards, or wire panels together just to see if your pods are crashing. Ghidorah intrinsically understands Kubernetes architecture and maps it instantly.

The Three Heads: A cluster is a living organism divided into three distinct nervous systems. Ghidorah visualizes them simultaneously:

The Left Head (Workloads): Compute and container state. What is running?

The Center Head (Network): Ingress, services, and connectivity. How is data flowing?

The Right Head (Infrastructure): Node health, CPU/Memory pressure, and bedrock stability. Where is it running?

Tactical Telemetry over "SaaS Slop": We believe monitoring tools should look like high-performance flight instruments, not generic web apps. High density, monospaced typography, and structural geometry. No wasted whitespace. No meaningless charts. Just pure, actionable relational data.

Zero-Friction Footprint: Built for the homelab, respected by production. Packaged as a single, ultra-lean binary, Ghidorah consumes a fraction of the resources of traditional observability stacks. Drop it into your cluster, and it breathes fire immediately.
